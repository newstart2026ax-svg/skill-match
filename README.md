# Skill Match

An app that starts from what a person can actually DO — their real skills, in plain words — and matches them to jobs that are verifiably open.

Not a job board. Not Indeed. A skill-to-work matcher.

## Who it's for
Hands-on workers whose skills outrun their resumes: kitchen and food service, trades, warehouse, care work, gig workers.

## How it works
1. The worker describes what they can do in plain language — no resume, no jargon. English and Spanish.
2. The app turns that into a skill profile, including transferable skills.
3. It matches that profile against live listings and shows what they're qualified for NOW — including adjacent jobs outside their current title — each with its source and when it was verified open.
4. Every result carries its source. If it can't be verified open, it doesn't get shown.

## The apply-direct rule
When the app matches a person with a job, they apply directly at the source. The app never reposts jobs. Expired postings are withheld — it would rather show fewer jobs than one dead one. Matched means move.

## Where the jobs come from
State agency listings first (Arizona state jobs, ARIZONA@WORK / AZ Job Connection), the National Labor Exchange (NLx), USAJobs, and CareerOneStop. Agency listings win ties — they're the verified layer.

## Status
Prototype, deployment ready. Arizona / Phoenix first. Open source.
